# cozy-house-game
## Cozy House Simulation

Welcome to your digital sanctuary. This is a highly immersive, interactive first-person relaxation sandbox designed to offer a peaceful, atmospheric escape. Step inside a beautifully rendered, modern one-story home where every detail is crafted to evoke comfort, quietude, and a total sense of calm.

Explore the living spaces, interact with the environment, and watch the world outside shift dynamically around you.

---

### Key Features

#### 📱 Fully Operational Smartphone
Your primary tool and companion is a functional in-game smartphone accessible right from your hotbar. 
*   **Live Weather Synchronization:** Features a built-in forecast application that tracks real-world meteorological data to mirror local climate conditions directly inside the simulation.
*   **Integrated Web Browser:** Open up a fully interactive web search experience directly inside the phone interface without losing sight of your surroundings.
*   **Dynamic Time Tracking:** Features an accurate digital clock interface that updates in real time to match system environments perfectly.

#### 💡 Independent Room Lighting Networks
Experience realistic home mechanics with completely separate electrical grids across different rooms.
*   **Tactile Light Switches:** Toggle the illumination profiles of the main bedroom and bathroom independently via local physical switches.
*   **Cozy Midnight Dimming:** Turning off the lights doesn't plunge the house into generic pitch darkness—instead, it smoothly shifts into a soft, calming night-mode glow.
*   **Natural Light Bleed:** Watch realistic light paths pour through open doorways and windows to create safe, warm, and inviting visual thresholds.

#### 🎛️ Layered Acoustic Audio Mix
Sound defines comfort. The simulation features a custom-mixed background audio environment specifically engineered to banish isolation and eliminate accidental tension:
*   **Mechanical Home Textures:** Ground yourself to a living environment with a steady, quiet HVAC and ceiling fan white-noise hum.
*   **Dynamic Weather Elements:** Listen to natural transitions like gentle wind through the property foliage or soft rain pattering against glass window panes based on your forecast status.
*   **Neighborhood Ambiance:** Enjoy a peaceful morning atmosphere featuring distant, soft backyard bird chirps mixed to perfection.

#### 🏡 Intentionally Detailed Architecture
*   **Interior Styling:** Features crisp wall-trim baseboards, beautifully textured stucco plaster detailing, and custom materials that respond organically to shadows and reflections.
*   **Spacious Yard Layout:** Walk outside the open doorway threshold onto a sprawling grassy lawn completely bordered by a secure perimeter fence.
*   **Deep Forest Backdrop:** Peer out the bedroom windows to view a perfectly spaced, sprawling background forest standing tall past your boundary line to frame the horizon.

---

### How to Play

*   **Move:** Walk freely around the house interior, bathroom suite, and exterior backyard lawns.
*   **Interact:** Interact with the environment around you—toggle light fixtures, look closer at objects, or pull up your items.
*   **Smartphone:** Activate your phone from the inventory hotbar to check the local weather, scroll the web, or review messages. 
*   **Relax:** There are no timers, no scores, and no threats. Use the space to unwind, listen to the background audio mix, and watch the afternoon sunlight or midnight shadows shift across the floors.
