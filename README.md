# [Game Title]

A charming and relaxing **first-person cozy house simulator** where you can unwind, explore a peaceful home environment, and interact with a fully functional smartphone. 

## 🎮 How to Play
* **Relax and Explore**: Take a peaceful stroll through the house or step outside into the fenced yard to enjoy the outdoors.
* **Interact with the Home**: Make yourself comfortable by picking up your phone from the nightstand, closing the window shutters, and exploring different rooms.
* **Browse the Real Web**: Pull up your in-game smartphone to check messages or open the browser to surf the actual, real-world internet while relaxing in your virtual space.

## 🕹️ Controls
* `W` `A` `S` `D` — **Move** around the house and yard
* `Arrow Keys` — **Look** around your surroundings
* `Shift` — **Sprint** to move a bit faster
* `Spacebar` — **Crouch** to get a cozy, lower perspective
* `E` — **Interact** with objects (Pick up your phone, close window shutters, open doors)
* `1` - `9` — **Hotbar Selection** (Switch between your flashlight, smartphone, and other items)

## ✨ Features
* **Fully Working Smartphone**: Pull up a realistic mobile interface complete with Phone, Messages, Weather, News, and a browser that lets you surf the real internet from inside the game.
* **Soothing Ambient Audio**: Unwind to a peaceful, layered soundtrack of chirping birds, a humming HVAC fan, a gently ticking clock, and the rustle of the wind.
* **Charming Visuals**: A clean, welcoming low-poly world paired with custom-textured walls and floors that give the house a warm, inviting feel.
* **Interactive Environment**: Secure your windows, toggle your flashlight, and explore at your own absolute pace with zero stress or rush.
